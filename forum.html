The Speakers

Featured Voices

The people in front of the room — and what they're bringing to it.

Dale Cooper
Founder  ·  Safe Slide Restoration & Cooper's Consulting
Opening  ·  Healthy Visionary  ·  EOS Panel  ·  MC

Dale built Safe Slide Restoration over thirty years from a regional outfit into a global operation — 400+ projects a year, an industry-recognized training program, and clients spanning waterparks on every continent. He also founded Cherrywood Farm & Golf Club. The Focus Forum was his idea, built for the business owners he's watched grind without the right framework for far too long. Husband to Cherry, father of 12.

Adam & Marie Hill
Professional EOS Implementer
Day One  ·  SPARK Session  ·  EOS Panel

Marie is a Relationship & Intimacy Coach who works with high-performing couples navigating the specific pressures that ambition puts on a marriage. Adam is a former fourth-generation CEO who scaled to 9 figures and grew revenue by $40M within two years of running EOS — and an Ironman finisher. Together they built the SPARK framework: a practical system for couples who lead together to rebuild trust, communicate honestly, and build a shared vision that actually holds up under the weight of a real business.

Ben Kelley
Certified EOS Implementer
Day Two  ·  VTO Flight Path  ·  EOS Panel

5+ years guiding leadership teams through EOS. Ben's background spans product design, real estate, and running a design company — a practitioner's lens on the same challenges his clients face. His focus: helping owners work less in the business and earn more, without burning out.

Marcus Truman
Leadership Performance Coach  ·  Archangel Performance
Day One  ·  Emotional Intelligence

Former Special Warfare leader with two decades of military service. Marcus coaches elite performers on mental resilience, emotional strength, and leading without losing yourself. He doesn't coach from theory — he coaches from the fire.

Mark McMullin
Attorney at Law  ·  Estate Planning & Elder Law
Day Two  ·  Legacy Planning

Founder of the Law Office of Mark McMullin in Cape Girardeau, MO. Mark began in finance before pursuing law at Mizzou — finding his calling in estate planning. He helps people be intentional about what happens to them, their family, and everything they've worked to build.

Alan Thomas
Software Engineer  ·  Smallstep
Day Two  ·  AI Panel

Builds the infrastructure and tooling that reliable systems run on. Alan's work spans certificate automation at Smallstep and LLM application development at Mirascope — practical experience constructing the systems that AI actually needs to function in the real world. He brings a builder's perspective to the session: what does it take to make these tools trustworthy, maintainable, and genuinely useful rather than just impressive.

Caleb Jones Shibu
Founding Software Engineer  ·  Page Technologies
Day Two  ·  AI Panel

Founding Software Engineer at Page Technologies, building the systems that make AI practical rather than theoretical. He brings a builder's perspective to the AI panel — focused on what it actually takes to deploy intelligent tools in real businesses and keep the human element intact when you do.

While You're Here

Explore St. Louis

You're in one of the most underrated cities in America. Use the time you have.

Driving advisory — I-44 through downtown St. Louis is currently closed due to a major sinkhole, with no confirmed reopening date. Check your navigation before heading in and give yourself extra time. Full story →

Tonight  ·  Eat & Drink
Ballpark Village

5-minute walk from the Hyatt. An entire entertainment district attached to Busch Stadium — Cardinals Nation restaurant, TOASTED bar, live music, outdoor patios. Electric on game nights and naturally where the city ends up. No reservation needed.

Tonight  ·  Watch
The Wheelhouse

8-minute walk. One of STL's most popular sports bars — hundreds of screens, solid food, great crowd. If the Cardinals are playing and you want to watch with the city rather than alone in your room, this is where to be.

See  ·  3 min walk
Gateway Arch

630 feet over the Mississippi — the view reaches 30 miles on a clear day. Trams depart every 15 minutes from the underground museum, 4-minute ride. Practically in your backyard.

— Book Tickets →
See
City Museum

Part playground, part art installation, part fever dream. An absolute must for anyone who thought they'd outgrown wonder. Go at night — it's a different experience entirely.

See
Forest Park

1,371 acres. The Art Museum, Zoo, and History Museum — all free. Bigger than Central Park. Come early, stay for hours, spend nothing.

Eat
The Hill

STL's Italian neighborhood. Old-school red sauce, bocce courts. Charlie Gitto's or Trattoria Marcella for the full experience. ~10 min from the hotel. (You'll also get a taste at Thursday's lunch.)

Eat
Broadway Oyster Bar

Live music nightly, Cajun and seafood, ice-cold drinks. A true St. Louis original. ~5 min drive from the Hyatt. No reservations — walk in, find a spot, stay too long.

Eat
Soulard

Cobblestones, live blues, and one of the oldest farmers markets in America running since 1779. McGurk's Irish Pub for a pint and a plate. The city's soul in one neighborhood.

Eat
Imo's Pizza

STL-style thin crust with Provel cheese. Controversial outside Missouri. Non-negotiable if you're in town. Multiple locations. It's fine — it's actually great.

Eat
Sugarfire Smoke House

Award-winning BBQ. Ribs, brisket, housemade sides. Multiple STL locations. The line moves. Worth it.

Watch
Cardinals at Busch Stadium

June is peak baseball. Busch Stadium is one of the most beautiful parks in the National League — walkable from the hotel. Check the schedule before you go. The city will be loud.

Our Charity

Day two closes with a presentation by The Hope Center Indy. Bring your generosity — donation via Venmo will be available at the event.

hopecenterindy.org →

The Hope Center Indy operates a 25-acre campus in Indianapolis serving women ages 18–30 coming out of sex trafficking, sexual exploitation, and addiction. Their mission: to impart hope and healing to every heart.

Through vocational training, therapy, and social enterprises — coffee shops and boutiques whose profits fund the mission — survivors are equipped for lives of independence and purpose. Mary Wilkinson will be presenting on behalf of Hope Center. The past does not define you. That's not a tagline there. It's the operating principle.
